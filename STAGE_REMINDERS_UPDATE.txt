STAGE REMINDERS (replaces the AI Schedule Assistant)
====================================================

REMOVED
  AI Schedule Assistant: ai_scheduler.py, AI_SCHEDULE_UPDATE.txt, the "AI Schedule"
  project tab and buttons, ai_schedule_* server actions, the openai package, and the
  LLM_* settings in render.yaml / .env.example. No OpenAI key is needed any more.

HOW IT WORKS
  Each pipeline step gets a time window (default 1 minute - demo setting).
  The clock starts when a client is added, and restarts every time it moves on.
  If the client is still at the same step when the window runs out, the person
  who must do the next hand-off gets a pop-up:
    - straight away when they log in / open the page, or within ~15 seconds
      if they are already signed in
    - with a beep, flashing tab title and a desktop notification (if allowed)

    Telecaller        -> "Send to Marketing TL"          (1 min)
    Marketing TL      -> "Verify & send to Manager"      (1 min)
    Marketing Manager -> "Approve & send to Accounts"    (1 min)
    Accounts          -> "Approve & send to Technical"   (1 min)
    Technical Mgr/TL, assigned writer / programmer / coordinator,
    Journal Manager/TL, proofreading / formatting / submission team ... every step.
  Steps that wait on the CLIENT's approval are not timed.

  Pop-up buttons
    Quick send     - one-click hand-off for the 4 marketing/accounts steps
    Open client    - opens the client; pop-up hides for the repeat time
    Cancel         - hides it; it comes back after 2 minutes if still pending

  Rejected and on-hold clients are not timed. When they are restored / resumed
  the step gets a fresh window.
  Clients that existed before this update are not timed until they next move.
  Clients bulk-imported from a sheet are not timed until they next move.

ADMIN (Super Admin / MD Admin) > Settings > Stage reminders
  - switch on/off
  - Time allowed per step (minutes)      default 1
  - Show again after Cancel (minutes)    default 2
  - "See overdue steps now": every late client and whose turn it is

DEMO (1-day deadline, Scopus paid)
  1. Telecaller adds the client (deadline = tomorrow), logs one call.
  2. Wait 1 minute without sending -> Telecaller gets the pop-up.
  3. Cancel -> it comes back after 2 minutes.
  4. Send to Marketing TL -> 1 minute later the Marketing TL gets the pop-up, and so on.
  Note: "Send to Marketing TL" still needs at least one logged call (existing rule).

DATABASE

  stage_reminder_snoozes.
