============================================================
 TASK BOARD: DATE & TIME FOR EVERY STAGE
============================================================

WHAT CHANGED
------------------------------------------------------------
Projects -> open a project -> Task Board.
Every stage the project has reached now shows, next to it:

    the date & time it reached that stage
    who sent it there (e.g. "sent here by Marketing TL")

    Lead entry (Telecaller)   15 Sept 2026, 10:05 am . added by Telecaller
    Marketing TL review       15 Sept 2026, 4:42 pm  . sent here by Telecaller
    Sent to Technical Team    Since 20 Sept 2026, 2:02 pm . sent here by
                              Accounts Team . 2 days so far

- The CURRENT stage says "Since ..." and how long it has been
  waiting there.
- If a project reached the same stage more than once (e.g. sent
  back for correction and returned), a small "x2" / "x3" badge
  appears. Hover over it to see every date & time.
- Future stages stay blank until they're reached.
- On phones the date goes under the stage name.

Nothing new has to be entered: the app already recorded every
stage change. Older projects show their dates straight away.
(A finished stage with no record - e.g. very old data - says
"No date recorded".)

Programmers / Paper Writers opening a project also see the
dates, but still NOT the internal stage notes. Clients don't
see this.


ALSO FIXED (existing bug)
------------------------------------------------------------
The Marketing TL's "Verify & send to Manager" button always
failed with "You don't have permission to do that". The server
had this step wrongly limited to the Technical roles. It now
works for the Marketing TL (and Marketing Manager / Admin).
