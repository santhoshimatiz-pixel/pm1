 PAPER WRITER: "MARK WRITING COMPLETED"
============================================================
Paper Writer -> Dashboard -> "Assigned to me - not yet submitted"

- The "Submit draft to Coordinator ->" button is REMOVED (from the
  card and from the client drawer).
- In its place: "Mark writing completed". It only records that the
  writing is done (date/time + who) - it doesn't send it anywhere.
- After marking, the card shows:
     Writing completed 24 Sept, 12:40 pm
     [Send it from My assigned tasks ->]  [Undo]
- "My assigned tasks" is UNCHANGED - the writer still sends it from
  there to Coordinator / Validation / Technical TL / Manager.
- When the Technical Manager / TL approves that task in Work
  Updates, the client now moves to WRITING_COMPLETE (Delivery) even
  though it never went through the old Coordinator pipeline step.
- The mark is cleared automatically when writing is (re)assigned.

FILES CHANGED: server.py, index.html.
Database: two new columns (writing_completed_at, writing_completed_by)
are added automatically on the next server start - nothing to do.
