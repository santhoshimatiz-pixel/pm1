CALL UPDATE
===========

WHAT CHANGED
------------
The Technical Manager / Technical TL dashboard has a "Call" button (top-right,
next to the page title). It opens a panel that:
- lets you pick ONE of two messages: "Come to cabin" or "Come to conference room"
  (no free text)
- lists the programmers and paper writers, with a green dot for anyone who is
  signed in right now
- has a "Call" button per person and a "Call everyone online" button
- shows the last 24 hours of calls with a status:
  Waiting for login -> Delivered -> Seen

EMPLOYEE SIDE
-------------
While an employee is signed in, their PM-tool tab checks for calls every
10 seconds in the background. A new call shows as:
- a DESKTOP notification from the browser, like a Gmail notification:
  title = who is calling (e.g. "Technical Manager"), text = the message,
  with the iMatiz icon. Visible while they are in Word, another tab or another
  app; stays on screen until clicked.
- a banner at the top of the PM tool with a "Got it" button
- a short beep and a flashing tab title
If the person is not signed in, the call is queued and appears when they log in.

Desktop notifications need the person's permission once: employees see a
"Turn on notifications" bar on their dashboard. Without it they still get the
in-page banner, beep and flashing title.
The PM tool must stay open in a browser tab (minimised is fine). In a
background tab the browser slows timers, so a call can take up to ~1 minute.

SECURITY
--------
Only Technical Manager, Technical TL and admins can call, and only their own
department's employees (server enforced). The server accepts only the two
fixed messages. Employees can only receive/acknowledge their own calls.
Sending is rate-limited to 60 per 5 minutes. New actions
(call_team_directory, call_employee, call_poll, call_ack) are in the
authorization matrix and use the normal session + CSRF checks.

DATABASE
--------
New table emp_calls, created automatically on start-up. No manual migration.

FILES CHANGED
-------------
server.py, index.html (and this note). No new packages.

SMALL EXTRA FIX (index.html)
----------------------------
After logging out or refreshing, Chrome sometimes re-filled a saved password
into the login box. The password / employee-ID / client-ID / captcha boxes are
now cleared automatically on page load, after logout and after a session
expiry, and the password box tells the browser not to autofill it.
