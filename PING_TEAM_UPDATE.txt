PING TEAM UPDATE
================

WHAT CHANGED
------------
The Technical Manager / Technical TL dashboard has a new "Ping team" button
(top-right, next to the page title). It opens a panel that:
- lists the programmers and paper writers, with a green dot for anyone who is
  signed in right now
- lets you type a short message (default: "Please check the PM tool.")
- has a "Ping" button per person and a "Ping everyone online" button
- shows the last 24 hours of pings with a status:
  Waiting for login -> Delivered -> Seen

EMPLOYEE SIDE
-------------
While an employee is signed in, their PM-tool tab checks for pings every
10 seconds in the background. A new ping shows as:
- a DESKTOP notification from the browser (visible while they are in Word,
  another tab or another app; stays on screen until clicked)
- a banner at the top of the PM tool with a "Got it" button
- a short beep and a flashing tab title
If the person is not signed in, the ping is queued and appears when they log in.

Desktop notifications need the person's permission once: employees see a
"Turn on notifications" bar on their dashboard. Without it they still get the
in-page banner, beep and flashing title.
The PM tool must stay open in a browser tab (minimised is fine). In a
background tab the browser slows timers, so a ping can take up to ~1 minute.

SECURITY
--------
Only Technical Manager, Technical TL and admins can send pings, and only to
their own department's employees (server enforced). Employees can only
receive/acknowledge their own pings. Messages are limited to 300 characters,
control characters are stripped, sending is rate-limited to 60 per 5 minutes.
New actions (ping_team_directory, ping_employee, ping_poll, ping_ack) are in
the authorization matrix and use the normal session + CSRF checks.

DATABASE
--------
New table emp_pings, created automatically on start-up. No manual migration.

FILES CHANGED
-------------
server.py, index.html (and this note). No new packages.
